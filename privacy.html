Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who uses it

AceSTestApps is used only by its owner, Lee, with his own Google account. It isn't available to the public.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: reads emails about work shifts and payments, and creates draft emails for invoices. It never sends email by itself.
  • Google Calendar: reads and updates Lee's own work calendars.

Where the data goes

All data is processed and stored on Lee's own computer and phone. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or sent to any other server.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local copies can be deleted from the owner's devices.

Contact

Use the developer contact email listed for this app in Google's sign-in screen.
